Method and reproduction

  1. Small-model fixed input: H.264, 1920×1080, 24 FPS; see release-manifest.json for its hash.
  2. Small-model cases add one channel per step, hold each step for 30 seconds, and use the second half as steady state.
  3. All 49 executed cases have independent sanitized reports and machine-readable data.

Results apply to the input, models, gates, and hold duration stated here.
